ACHAEMENID PERSIA
IN FILM & SERIES

From Cyrus the Great to Alexander's conquest

550 BC - 330 BC
Phase I
550 - 539 BC
Cyrus the Great Rises
Cyrus unites the Persians and Medes - conquest of Lydia - the empire is born
DOC Art of Persia (2020)
DOC Engineering an Empire: The Persians (2006)
⟡ No major drama covers Cyrus directly - documentary bridge required.
Phase II
539 - 530 BC
Babylon and Imperial Foundations
Cyrus captures Babylon - imperial administration expands - the Persian model of tolerance takes shape
DOC Art of Persia (2020)
DOC Engineering an Empire: The Persians (2006)
⟡ Documentary bridge recommended for the empire's early administrative era.
Phase III
530 - 486 BC
Cambyses, Darius, and Imperial Reform
Cambyses conquers Egypt - Darius I restores order - satrapies, the Royal Road, and a global imperial system
DOC Art of Persia (2020)
DOC Engineering an Empire: The Persians (2006)
FILM One Night with the King (2006)
⟡ One Night with the King dramatizes a later Achaemenid court (Esther/Xerxes).
Phase IV
486 - 479 BC
Xerxes and the Greek Wars
Thermopylae and Salamis - Persian power confronts the Greek city-states - the wars become Western cinematic mythology
FILM 300 (2006)
FILM 300: Rise of an Empire (2014)
FILM The 300 Spartans (1962)
⟡ These films are Greek-centered and highly stylized - watch for spectacle, not accuracy.
⟡ Use Art of Persia for a Persian-sympathetic counterbalance.
Phase V
479 - 404 BC
Imperial Continuity and Court Power
Post-war consolidation - Artaxerxes I and the long imperial middle - satrap politics and diplomacy
DOC Art of Persia (2020)
FILM One Night with the King (2006)
⟡ Documentary bridge recommended for the quieter imperial middle.
Phase VI
404 - 330 BC
Late Achaemenids and the Macedonian Storm
Satrap revolts and succession strains - Artaxerxes III and Darius III - Alexander's conquest ends the dynasty
FILM Alexander (2004)
FILM Alexander the Great (1956)
⟡ Use these as the Macedonian-view of Persia's fall; see macedon.html for full Alexander context.
