THE MAURYA EMPIRE
IN FILM & SERIES

From Chandragupta's coup to Ashoka's zenith and the long decline

322 BC - 185 BC
Phase I
326 - 322 BC
The Macedonian Shockwave
Alexander's invasion shakes the northwest - local kingdoms resist - the political vacuum sets the stage for Chandragupta
SERIES Porus (2017)
⟡ Use Porus as the prelude to Mauryan emergence.
Phase II
322 - 305 BC
Chandragupta's Coup and the Mauryan Founding
Overthrow of the Nanda dynasty - alliance with Chanakya - unification of Magadha - capital at Pataliputra - the Mauryan state is born
SERIES Chanakya (1991)
SERIES Chandragupta Maurya (2011)
SERIES Chandra Nandini (2016)
⟡ Chanakya is the political blueprint series.
⟡ Chandragupta Maurya (2011) is the core dramatization.
⟡ Chandra Nandini offers an alternate court perspective.
Character Spotlight - Chandragupta and Chanakya
Chandragupta's rise is inseparable from Chanakya's statecraft. The 2011 series dramatizes the coup, the alliance, and the first imperial reforms with the most narrative depth.
Phase III
305 - 298 BC
Treaty with Seleucus and Consolidation
War on the northwest frontier - treaty and marriage alliance with Seleucus I - expansion into the Indus region - imperial bureaucracy deepens
SERIES Chandragupta Maurya (2011)
SERIES Chandragupta Maurya (2018)
⟡ Use the two Chandragupta series as parallel takes on the consolidation era.
Phase IV
298 - 273 BC
Bindusara and Southern Expansion
Bindusara inherits the empire - campaigns into the Deccan - court diplomacy with Hellenistic states - Ashoka rises within the court
SERIES Chakravartin Ashoka Samrat (2015)
⟡ Early episodes cover Bindusara's reign and Ashoka's youth.
Phase V
268 - 261 BC
Ashoka's Conquests and the Kalinga War
Ashoka seizes the throne - rapid military campaigns - the brutal Kalinga War - the moral crisis that reshapes the empire
FILM Asoka (2001)
FILM Samrat Ashoka (1992)
SERIES Chakravartin Ashoka Samrat (2015)
⟡ Asoka (2001) captures the Kalinga pivot.
⟡ The 2015 series adds the political depth around Ashoka's coup.
Character Spotlight - Ashoka the Great
Ashoka's transformation after Kalinga is the Mauryan story's emotional core. The 2001 film is the cinematic centerpiece, while the 2015 series offers the most complete arc.
Phase VI
260 - 232 BC
The Dharma Empire
Ashoka embraces Buddhism - edicts carved across the subcontinent - missions spread the faith - the empire pivots from conquest to moral rule
SERIES Chakravartin Ashoka Samrat (2015)
FILM Asoka (2001)
⟡ Use the series for governance and edict reforms, then revisit Asoka for the moral arc.
Phase VII
232 - 185 BC
Succession Strains and the Shunga Coup
Ashoka's successors struggle to hold the vast realm - provincial governors gain power - Pushyamitra Shunga ends the dynasty in 185 BC
SERIES Chakravartin Ashoka Samrat (2015)
⟡ Late episodes give the closest dramatization to the dynasty's final collapse.
