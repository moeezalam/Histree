THE OTTOMAN EMPIRE
IN FILM & SERIES

From frontier beylik to the fall of 1922

1299 AD - 1922 AD
Phase I
1299 - 1453
Founding and Expansion
Osman and the frontier beylik - early conquests in Anatolia and the Balkans - the empire approaches Constantinople
SERIES Dirilis: Ertugrul (2014)
⟡ Dirilis: Ertugrul is the definitive pre-empire origin story.
⟡ Link to rome.html for the Byzantine context of 1453.
Phase II
1453
Mehmed II and the Conquest of Constantinople
53-day siege - massive cannons breach the Theodosian Walls - Constantinople falls and a new imperial capital rises
SERIES Rise of Empires: Ottoman (2020)
FILM Fetih 1453 (2012)
⟡ Rise of Empires: Ottoman is the primary, most accurate treatment.
⟡ Fetih 1453 is the cinematic epic companion.
Phase III
1520 - 1566
Suleiman the Magnificent
The imperial golden age - legal reforms, court intrigue, and military dominance across three continents
SERIES Magnificent Century (2011)
⟡ The backbone series for the Ottoman golden age.
Phase IV
1600 - 1700s
Palace Politics and Stabilization
Court factionalism and the power of the Valide sultans - a slower, internal-focused empire
SERIES Magnificent Century: Kosem (2015)
⟡ Kosem covers the complex dynastic politics of the 17th century.
Phase V
1839 - 1909
Tanzimat and Late Imperial Reform
Modernization under pressure - Abdulhamid II's rule - surveillance and reform at the empire's edge
SERIES Payitaht: Abdulhamid (2017)
⟡ The late reform era is best represented here.
Phase VI
1914 - 1922
World War I and Collapse
Gallipoli, Mesopotamia, and the Arab Revolt - imperial collapse after the Great War - the sultanate ends in 1922
FILM The Ottoman Lieutenant (2017)
MINI Gallipoli (2015)
⟡ Gallipoli is the battlefield lens; Ottoman Lieutenant gives an internal view.
