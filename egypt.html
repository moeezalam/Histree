THE HISTORY OF EGYPT
IN FILM & SERIES

From New Kingdom glory to Cleopatra's fall

1550 BC - 30 BC
Phase I
1550 - 1353 BC
New Kingdom Ascendance
Ahmose I expels the Hyksos - Hatshepsut and Thutmose III expand Egypt - imperial wealth peaks in Thebes and Karnak
DOC Egypt's Golden Empire (2001)
⟡ Egypt's Golden Empire is the primary New Kingdom overview.
Phase II
1353 - 1336 BC
Akhenaten and Nefertiti
Religious revolution of Aten worship - Amarna capital - Nefertiti's political ascendancy
FILM Nefertiti: Daughter of the Sun (1994)
DOC Egypt's Golden Empire (2001)
⟡ Use Nefertiti (1994) for the dramatized court; the doc fills gaps.
Phase III
1332 - 1323 BC
Tutankhamun and Restoration
The boy king reverses Aten worship - power struggles in the court - the fragile restoration of Amun
MINI Tut (2015)
⟡ The only modern dramatization centered on Tut's court.
Phase IV
1290 - 1213 BC
Ramesside Age and the Exodus Tradition
Ramses II and monumental building - war with the Hittites - the Exodus tradition and Egypt's most famous cinematic myth
FILM The Ten Commandments (1956)
FILM The Prince of Egypt (1998)
FILM Exodus: Gods and Kings (2014)
⟡ Ten Commandments first for the classic epic baseline.
⟡ Prince of Egypt is the most emotionally precise retelling.
⟡ Exodus (2014) is modern spectacle with looser accuracy.
Phase V
1189 - 1070 BC
Late New Kingdom Decline
Internal corruption and priestly power - Sea Peoples pressure - the empire fractures after the Ramesside line
FILM Pharaoh (1966)
⟡ Pharaoh (1966) is a political tragedy about collapse and reform.
Phase VI
1070 - 664 BC
Third Intermediate Fragmentation
Libyan dynasties, Kushite revival, and regional rule - Egypt oscillates between unity and fragmentation
DOC Egypt Uncovered (1998)
⟡ Documentary bridge recommended for this thinly dramatized era.
Phase VII
664 - 332 BC
Late Period and Foreign Dominations
Saite revival - Assyrian and Persian pressures - Egypt loses autonomy before Alexander's arrival
DOC Egypt Uncovered (1998)
⟡ Documentary bridge recommended for the Late Period.
Phase VIII
332 - 30 BC
Ptolemaic Egypt and Cleopatra
Greek rule after Alexander - dynastic intrigue - Cleopatra VII allies with Caesar and Antony - Actium and Roman annexation
FILM Cleopatra (1963)
MINI Cleopatra (1999)
⟡ The 1963 epic is the definitive screen Cleopatra.
⟡ The 1999 miniseries adds political detail and context.
⟡ Continue to rome.html for Caesar and Antony's parallel arcs.
